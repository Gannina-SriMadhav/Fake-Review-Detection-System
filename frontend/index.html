AI review authenticity analysis

Is that review real?

Paste any product review. Veritas estimates how closely it resembles genuine or fake writing, shows how sure it is, and explains why.

How it works

Four steps, usually under half a second.

You paste a reviewThe text is cleaned (HTML, spacing) but your wording, punctuation and style are kept - they are clues.
Models read the writingEach model looks at word choice, phrases, character patterns and style, and compares them with ~40,000 reviews it was taught from.
Confidence is calibratedThe raw score is corrected so "80%" really means about 80% of such cases were right on test data. No fake precision.
You get the evidenceThe verdict, a confidence level, which words pushed it each way, and a check for near-copies of known reviews.
🛡️Read this once: the result is a statistical estimate, never proof. Short or unusual reviews give weak evidence and are flagged LOW confidence. The model was taught to tell computer-generated reviews from human ones, so it isn't validated on paid human fakes. English only: reviews in other languages are detected and flagged as unreliable.
