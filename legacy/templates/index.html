About Fake Review Detection System

AI & Natural Language Processing Review Authenticity Engine

🎯 Mission & Purpose

Fake Review Detection System is an AI platform designed to detect deceptive and fraudulent reviews on e-commerce platforms and online services. Using NLP techniques and Machine Learning (RoBERTa Transformer + TF-IDF), it analyzes sentiment, phrase repetition, and vocabulary patterns to classify reviews as Genuine or Fake.

🤖 Machine Learning Architecture

Powered by a fine-tuned RoBERTa (Transformer) classifier paired with a TF-IDF N-gram classifier for syntactic feature extraction. The pipeline achieves high accuracy across product and service domains.

📁 Datasets & Training

Trained on curated datasets of genuine and fraudulent product/service reviews (Fake_Reviews.csv & Genuine_Reviews.csv) across electronics, clothing, hospitality, and digital services.

🔒 Analytics & Session Security

Includes user authentication, isolated user prediction history, Plotly analytical distribution charts, and SQLite database persistence.
